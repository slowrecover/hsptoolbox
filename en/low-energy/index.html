What to do on a low-energy day

On low-energy days the hardest part is often the decision: whether you feel like it, whether it is worth it, whether you can manage the full version. That debate uses up the little energy there is.

The Low-Energy System takes the debate away. Each basic habit has a floor version, the smallest form that still counts, such as sitting up for thirty seconds or reading one page. You don't ask whether you feel like it. You do the floor version and tick it.

How to use it

  1. Pick any item on the list.
  2. Do only its floor version. Doing more is fine, but never required.
  3. Tap the item to tick it. Your ticks are saved only in this browser.

If low energy lasts for weeks, or comes with changes in mood, sleep or appetite, it is worth talking to a doctor.
