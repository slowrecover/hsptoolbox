How to choose when everything feels important

When a long list feels equally urgent, it is easy to spend the day switching between items or worrying about all of them at once. Find the Main Thing slows that down. You write everything out, then look at each item through four questions:

  • Consequence: will delaying it cost you something important in three months?
  • Leverage: would finishing it make other things easier or unnecessary?
  • Real urgency: does it truly need action now, or is it just loud?
  • Action: can you take a real step on it today?

The item that passes the most filters becomes your main thing for today. If two are tied, you choose with your gut. Everything else is parked for later, not cancelled.
