What does "highly sensitive" mean?

High sensitivity, also called sensory processing sensitivity, is a temperament trait first described by psychologist Elaine Aron in the 1990s. Highly sensitive people tend to process things deeply, and can be overwhelmed more easily by noise, pressure or a packed schedule. It is a trait, not a disorder.

These tools are built for the moments that trait can make harder: starting, choosing, and talking to yourself kindly when things go wrong.

How the tools are different

  • Private by design. What you write stays on your device. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded.
  • One problem each. Every tool does a single job and takes a minute or two.
  • No forced positivity. The tools help you look at a thought or a task more clearly. They don't tell you how to feel.

Built by someone who knows these moments firsthand. Open source on GitHub. 中文版工具箱 has more tools in Chinese.

These tools are for everyday overwhelm. They are not a substitute for professional care. If you are struggling to stay safe, you can find a free, confidential helpline in your country at findahelpline.com.
