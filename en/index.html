What this is

Small tools I built with AI for myself during a long low stretch, now shared for anyone who needs them. Free, no sign-up, nothing uploaded: what you write stays on your device. High sensitivity is a temperament trait first described by psychologist Elaine Aron, not a disorder.

These are everyday self-help tools, not medical tools, and not a substitute for professional care. If you are struggling to stay safe, contact your local emergency number or find a free, confidential helpline at findahelpline.com. Made for the me I used to be. · Open source on GitHub
